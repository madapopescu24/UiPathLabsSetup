    UiPath Partner Success · UiPath Labs

    🧪 UiPath Labs Setup¶

    Get your UiPath Labs environment ready for a workshop: your VM, your workshop account, and your tenant. Work through the tabs from left to right — each one ends with a check.

    Training Environment

    Log in at https://cloud.uipath.com/tpenlabs with your workshop account. You work in the tenants AgenticWorkshop and AgenticTestingWorkshop.

    🎁 What UiPath Labs gives you¶

    Everyone in the workshop gets the same ready-made environment, so nobody spends the session installing tools.

    🖥️ A Windows VM

    A virtual machine (VM) you open in your browser. Chrome, UiPath Studio, and the tools the workshop uses are already installed.

    👤 A workshop account

    A UiPath account made for the workshop. You sign in with it instead of your own account.

    🏢 Two workshop tenants

    The tenants AgenticWorkshop and AgenticTestingWorkshop, where Test Manager, Studio Web, and Orchestrator are ready for the exercises.
