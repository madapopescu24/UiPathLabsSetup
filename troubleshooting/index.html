    🛠️ Troubleshooting¶

    Look up your symptom here before you raise your hand. If it isn't listed, or the fix doesn't work, ask the trainer.

    🖥️ The VM¶

    🚨 Ctrl+V doesn't paste into the VM in Azure Bastion

    Cause Chrome on your own machine blocks the Bastion site from your clipboard

    Fix Open chrome://settings/content/clipboard on your own machine, remove the Bastion site from Not allowed to see your clipboard, and reload the tab

    🚨 Signing in to the VM fails

    Cause The username or password isn't the VM's, for example the workshop account's, or the password was typed by hand

    Fix Enter the Username from Sandbox sign-in (admin_1), paste the Password with its copy button, and try again

    🔑 Signing in¶

    🚨 You're signed in with your own account

    Cause Chrome reused an account it already knew

    Fix Sign out, then sign in with Continue with Microsoft and the Username from UiPath sign-in

    🚨 A workshop tenant is missing from the Tenant picker

    Cause You're signed in with another account, or your workshop account wasn't added to that tenant

    Fix Check that you signed in with the Username from UiPath sign-in. If AgenticWorkshop or AgenticTestingWorkshop is still missing, tell the trainer

    🚨 You're on another tenant

    Cause The account opened its last-used tenant

    Fix In the Tenant picker, in the upper-right corner, switch to the tenant the trainer names: AgenticWorkshop or AgenticTestingWorkshop

    🚨 Studio Web goes blank or shows an old screen

    Cause A known Studio Web display issue

    Fix Open Studio Web in a fresh tab, and check the Tenant picker shows the tenant for your workshop

    Still stuck?

    Raise your hand. The trainer can check your VM, your workshop account, and your tenant access.
